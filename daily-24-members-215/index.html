SAT OCT 10, 2026 DATING

How do I tell someone I'm dating that I'm in recovery?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

You like them and you do not want to scare them off. You are also tired of hiding a big part of your life, and you do not know when the right time is.

THE ANSWER

You do not owe it on the first date. You do owe it before it gets serious, and before the person is making decisions that depend on it.

1 — PICK A CALM MOMENT, NOT A PEAK ONE. Not in the middle of a fight, not after a drink order, not on the way out the door. A walk or a quiet dinner works.

2 — SAY IT SHORT AND PLAIN. "I'm in recovery. I don't drink and I'm serious about it. I wanted you to hear it from me." You do not owe the whole history yet.

3 — LET THEM RESPOND. Do not fill the silence and do not apologize. Give them a minute to take it in.

4 — WATCH WHAT THEY DO NEXT. Respect, a question, a joke at your expense, or pressure to just have one. All of that tells you something you need to know early.

5 — KEEP YOUR BOUNDARIES SPECIFIC. No drinking at your place, no bar dates for now, and you leave when you want to. A good partner works with that.

Telling the truth early is the cheapest filter you will ever have.

DO THIS TODAY

Write the one sentence you would say, out loud, once, so it sounds like you and not like a confession.

WATCH OUT FOR

Waiting so long that telling them feels like a betrayal. Late is harder than early.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
FRI OCT 9, 2026 RANDOM-URGE

What do I do when the urge hits out of nowhere and nothing triggered it?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

You were fine, you were having a normal day, and then it showed up at full volume. It makes you think something is wrong with you or that the recovery is not working.

THE ANSWER

Out of nowhere almost always has a something. You just did not catch it yet. The urge is not a verdict on your recovery.

1 — TREAT IT LIKE ANY OTHER URGE. Move, cold water, rate it 1 to 10, tell one person. The steps do not change because you cannot explain it.

2 — CHECK THE FOUR BASICS. When did you last eat, sleep, talk to a person, and get outside? Missing two of those makes almost any urge louder.

3 — LOOK BACK TWO HOURS. A song, a smell, a text, a car that looked like somebody's, an argument you forgot about. Your brain logs triggers faster than you notice them.

4 — WRITE DOWN THE TIME AND WHAT YOU WERE DOING. After a few of these, a pattern shows up. A pattern you can see is a pattern you can plan around.

Surprise urges are common, and they get farther apart. Having one does not erase the days you have built.

DO THIS TODAY

Set a note on your phone called URGES. Every time one hits, log the time, what you were doing, and the number from 1 to 10.

WATCH OUT FOR

Reading the urge as proof you are failing. It is a signal, and signals pass.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
THU OCT 8, 2026 COURT-DATE

How do I get through a court date or a probation check without spiraling?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

The date on the calendar takes over your head for days. You replay every way it can go wrong, and the stress is exactly the kind of pressure the old habit was built to handle.

THE ANSWER

The day itself is smaller than the dread leading up to it. Most of the damage people do is in the days before, not in the room.

1 — KNOW EXACTLY WHAT HAPPENS. Time, place, who you check in with, what you bring. Write it down. Most of the spiral lives in not knowing.

2 — PREPARE THE BORING PARTS THE NIGHT BEFORE. Clothes, paperwork, ID, how you get there, when you leave. Leave early. Late is the one thing fully in your control.

3 — TELL ONE PERSON THE DATE. Ask them to text you that morning and again after. You are not alone in it and somebody will notice if you go quiet.

4 — PLAN THE HOUR AFTER, NOT JUST THE HOUR OF. The relief or the bad news both hit afterward. Have somewhere to be and somebody to see right when you walk out.

5 — KEEP YOUR ANSWERS SHORT AND TRUE. Do not perform and do not over-explain. Yes sir, no sir, and the facts.

Whatever happens, you handle the next day the same way: eat, move, tell somebody, do one normal thing.

DO THIS TODAY

Write the date, time, address and what you need to bring on one piece of paper and put it by your keys.

WATCH OUT FOR

Treating the night before as the time to relax with something. That is the night to be boring, fed, and in bed.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
WED OCT 7, 2026 OLD-FRIEND

What do I do when a friend from the old life reaches out?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

They are not a stranger and they are not necessarily a bad person. That is what makes it hard. You miss them, you feel rude ignoring them, and you are not sure which of the two of you has changed.

THE ANSWER

You can love somebody and still not be able to be around them yet. Both are true at the same time.

1 — ASK YOURSELF ONE QUESTION FIRST. If we hang out, what do we do? If the honest answer involves a bar, a dealer, or a house where it happens, you have your answer before they finish the text.

2 — REPLY, DON'T VANISH. A short true message beats silence. "Good to hear from you. I'm not drinking right now, so I'm keeping my distance from the old spots." That is the whole message.

3 — IF YOU WANT TO SEE THEM, CHANGE THE SETTING. Breakfast, the gym, a drive, a game. Daytime, short, with your own car and a time you leave.

4 — WATCH WHAT THEY DO WITH THE NEW VERSION OF YOU. A real friend says good for you. Someone who needs you back in the old routine gets quiet, jokes about it, or pushes.

You are allowed to say not yet. Not yet is not never, and it is not an insult.

DO THIS TODAY

Write your one short reply now, even if you do not send it today, so it is ready when they text again.

WATCH OUT FOR

Saying yes just to be nice and deciding the rest later. Later arrives at the exact place you were trying to avoid.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
TUE OCT 6, 2026 WORKOUT

How do I start working out when I'm still detoxing and exhausted?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Everybody tells you exercise helps and your body currently cannot climb a flight of stairs without wanting to lie down, so the advice feels like it's for a different person than the one you are right now.

THE ANSWER

The workout that helps you right now is not the one that would have impressed you a year ago. It's the smallest one that still counts.

1 — WALK BEFORE YOU LIFT ANYTHING. Ten minutes outside. That's the whole workout on the days your body is still clearing out. It still counts.

2 — LET TIRED BE THE PLAN, NOT THE EXCUSE. You are not skipping it because you're tired. You're doing the tired version of it instead: slower, shorter, still happening.

3 — MOVE AT THE SAME TIME EVERY DAY. The consistency matters more than the intensity right now. Your body is learning a rhythm again, not chasing a personal record.

4 — STOP THE SECOND SOMETHING FEELS WRONG, NOT JUST HARD. Hard is fine. Dizzy, chest pain, or genuinely sick is not. Your body is doing real repair work underneath this and it deserves you listening to it.

Six months from now the version of you lifting real weight started on a ten minute walk you almost didn't do. That's not a smaller win, that's the actual first one.

DO THIS TODAY

Put on shoes and walk for ten minutes today, at the same time you plan to do it tomorrow.

WATCH OUT FOR

Comparing this workout to the one you used to do before everything fell apart. That's a different body carrying a different day.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
MON OCT 5, 2026 APPETITE

What do I eat when my appetite is wrecked?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Nothing sounds good, eating feels like a chore on top of everything else you're managing, and skipping meals feels easier right up until it makes everything else harder too.

THE ANSWER

You do not need appetite to eat. You need a plan simple enough that hunger isn't required to follow it.

1 — EAT ON A CLOCK, NOT ON HUNGER. Three set times a day whether you're hungry or not. Waiting to feel hungry in early recovery can mean waiting a long time.

2 — PICK THREE MEALS YOU CAN MAKE WITHOUT THINKING. Eggs and toast. A sandwich. A frozen meal you actually like. Rotate them. Decision fatigue is real and food shouldn't be another decision.

3 — DRINK SOMETHING WITH CALORIES IF FOOD WON'T GO DOWN. A protein shake, milk, juice. It is not a real meal and it is better than nothing on a day nothing else works.

4 — EAT WITH SOMEONE WHEN YOU CAN. Appetite comes back faster around other people eating than it does sitting alone staring at a plate.

Your appetite will come back before your motivation to cook does. Don't wait for either one, just eat the plan.

DO THIS TODAY

Pick your three no-think meals right now and write them somewhere you'll see at mealtime.

WATCH OUT FOR

Replacing food with caffeine or cigarettes because they're easier. That trades one problem for a new one.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
SUN OCT 4, 2026 PAYDAY

How do I handle payday when payday used to mean using?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

The check hits your account and your body remembers what used to happen next before your brain even catches up, and that muscle memory doesn't care how many days you have.

THE ANSWER

Payday is not a trigger you wait out. It's a day you plan for like a schedule, not a feeling.

1 — MOVE MONEY OUT BEFORE YOU FEEL ANYTHING. Automatic transfer to savings, or to whoever's holding money for you right now, the second it lands. Decide the amount days before, not the moment it hits.

2 — HAVE THE FIRST TWO HOURS PLANNED. Gym, a meeting, dinner with someone. The danger window is short. Fill it on purpose.

3 — TELL SOMEONE IT'S PAYDAY. Not for permission. So one other person knows today is a harder day than most, without you having to explain why later.

4 — SPEND ON SOMETHING REAL THE SAME DAY. Groceries, a bill, something for the apartment. Give the money a job before boredom gives it a different one.

You are not fighting the craving on payday. You are out-scheduling it. A full calendar beats willpower every time.

DO THIS TODAY

Set the automatic transfer up right now, before the next check lands, not the morning of.

WATCH OUT FOR

Carrying more cash than you need "just in case." Just in case is exactly the case you're trying to avoid.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
SAT OCT 3, 2026 PARTNER-PAST

What do I do when my partner keeps bringing up the past?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

You feel like you're doing everything right now, and every fight still turns into a list of everything you did before, and it feels like nothing you do today will ever be enough to close it out.

THE ANSWER

They are not bringing up the past to punish you. They are checking if it's actually over, because your word alone stopped being proof a while ago.

1 — DO NOT ARGUE THE TIMELINE. "That was two years ago" is true and it will not land, because their fear isn't about the calendar, it's about whether it happens again.

2 — ANSWER THE FEAR UNDER THE COMPLAINT. When it comes up, try: "I get why you're still scared. Here's what's different now," and name one real thing, not a promise.

3 — LET THEM BRING IT UP WITHOUT SHUTTING IT DOWN. Every time you get defensive it teaches them not to trust you with it, which is the opposite of what you want.

4 — BUILD THE RECORD, DON'T ARGUE THE RECORD. Time and consistency are what actually change this. A good conversation tonight does not out-argue eighteen months of history.

You cannot talk your way out of something you have to live your way out of. That takes longer than either of you wants, and it's still the only road there is.

DO THIS TODAY

Next time it comes up, answer the fear instead of the accusation. One sentence, no defense attached.

WATCH OUT FOR

Keeping score of how long it's been like it's a debt they owe you for. That turns your own progress into a weapon.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
FRI OCT 2, 2026 WHITE-KNUCKLING

How do I stop white-knuckling and actually want this?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

You are staying clean through pure force, gripping it with both hands every single day, and you're exhausted, because willpower was never built to run a whole life.

THE ANSWER

Wanting it does not arrive by waiting. It gets built the same way muscle does, through repeated small reps, not through one big decision.

1 — STOP RELYING ON WILLPOWER FOR EVERYTHING. Willpower is for the moment the craving hits. Build routines that don't need it at all: same wake time, same meeting, same gym slot.

2 — FIND ONE THING THAT'S ACTUALLY BETTER NOW. Not "everything's better," one specific thing. You sleep. You have money on Tuesday. Write it down when you notice it.

3 — STOP MEASURING TODAY AGAINST USING-DAYS. You are comparing a hard, boring Tuesday to a memory your own brain has already edited to remove the bad parts. That is not a fair fight.

4 — LET WANTING IT COME LATER. Some days you do this because you decided to, not because you want to. That still counts. The wanting shows up after enough days like that, not before.

White-knuckling is what day 10 looks like. Wanting it is what day 200 looks like. You are not failing by not being there yet.

DO THIS TODAY

Write down the one specific thing that is better now, today, before you do anything else.

WATCH OUT FOR

Waiting to feel motivated before you do the next right thing. The motivation is downstream of the action, not upstream of it.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
THU OCT 1, 2026 FIRST-SHARE

What do I say in my first meeting when they ask me to share?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

The chair looks at you and the room goes quiet and your brain tells you whatever comes out of your mouth next has to be right, which is exactly the kind of pressure that makes people say nothing at all.

THE ANSWER

Nobody in that room is grading you. Most of them can barely remember their own first share.

1 — SAY YOUR NAME AND PASS IF YOU NEED TO. "I'm Mike, I'll just listen today" is a complete, acceptable share. Passing the first time is normal, not a failure.

2 — IF YOU DO TALK, SAY WHERE YOU ARE, NOT YOUR WHOLE STORY. "I'm three days in and it's rough" is enough. You do not owe the room your history on day one.

3 — BORROW A SENTENCE FROM SOMEONE ELSE WHO SPOKE. "What she just said about the mornings being the hardest, that's me too" counts as sharing and it is easier than starting from nothing.

4 — STOP WHEN YOU RUN OUT OF WORDS. There is no minimum length. Ten seconds that are true beats two minutes of filler.

The room does not need your best share. It needs you back next week. That is the only thing that actually helps you.

DO THIS TODAY

Before the meeting, decide which of the four you're doing so you are not deciding it live.

WATCH OUT FOR

Rehearsing something impressive in your head while other people talk. You will miss what they said and still feel stuck when your turn comes.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
WED SEP 30, 2026 HOLIDAY

How do I handle a wedding, a funeral, or a holiday party sober?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

These events are not built around you. They are built around an open bar and a room full of people who have not thought about your sobriety for one second, so you walk in without a plan and let the room set the terms.

THE ANSWER

You are not the only sober person who has ever survived one of these. You just have not built the version of you that walks in with a plan yet.

1 — DRIVE YOURSELF. Your own car is your own exit. Never ride with someone whose night is not yours to control.

2 — HOLD A DRINK THAT LOOKS LIKE A DRINK. Soda water with lime in a real glass. Nobody asks a person holding a glass what's in it.

3 — PICK YOUR PERSON BEFORE YOU WALK IN. One relative, one old friend, whoever you can stand next to when it gets loud. Text them before you arrive: I might need to leave early, stick with me.

4 — GIVE YOURSELF A TIME LIMIT, NOT A GOAL. Ninety minutes is a full appearance. You do not have to stay until the end to prove anything.

You are allowed to leave your own cousin's wedding at 8pm. Nobody remembers who left early. Everybody remembers who got messy.

DO THIS TODAY

Text your exit person the plan tonight, before the event, not in the parking lot.

WATCH OUT FOR

Toasting with whatever's in your hand because everyone else is raising a glass. Raise the soda water. Nobody is checking what's in it, you are.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
TUE SEP 29, 2026 SLEEP

How do I sleep when I can't sleep sober?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Sleep is the last thing to come back and the first thing that wrecks you when it is gone. Most people are told to be patient and given nothing to actually do.

THE ANSWER

Your sleep is not broken forever. It takes weeks to months to reset, and how you run the day decides how fast.

1 — SAME WAKE TIME, EVERY SINGLE DAY. Not the same bedtime, the same WAKE time, weekends included. That is the one lever that resets the whole clock, and it is the one people skip.

2 — GET SUNLIGHT IN YOUR EYES WITHIN AN HOUR OF WAKING. Ten minutes outside, no sunglasses. It sets the timer that makes you sleepy about 14 hours later.

3 — TRAIN, BUT NOT LATE. Hard effort in the day, nothing heavy in the last 3 hours before bed.

4 — NO CAFFEINE AFTER NOON. In early recovery most people replace one thing with coffee and then wonder why 1am is loud.

5 — IF YOU ARE AWAKE 20 MINUTES, GET UP. Lying there teaches your body that bed is where you lie awake. Get up, dim light, boring thing, back when heavy.

6. NO PHONE IN THE BEDROOM. Not on the nightstand. Another room. The 2am scroll is where a lot of people talk themselves into things.

Expect vivid dreams, including using dreams. They are common, they are not a sign you are slipping, and they fade.

DO THIS TODAY

Set one alarm for the same time every morning this week, including Saturday and Sunday. Do not set a bedtime. Just the wake time.

WATCH OUT FOR

Reaching for something to knock yourself out. Swapping one thing you cannot stop for another is not sleep, it is a new problem with a better reputation.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
MON SEP 28, 2026 ANGER

What do I do when I'm angry and my old fix was the only thing that touched it?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Anger is the one that gets people at six months. Sadness sends you to a meeting. Anger sends you straight to the thing, because it worked fast and it worked every time.

THE ANSWER

You never learned to be angry sober. That is not a character problem, it is a missing skill, and it is learnable.

1 — BURN IT THROUGH YOUR BODY FIRST. Anger is chemical before it is anything else. Heavy lifting, a hard walk, a bag if you have one. Twenty minutes of real output and the volume drops enough to think.

2 — DO NOT MAKE ANY DECISION FOR ONE HOUR. No texts, no calls, no quitting, no showing up anywhere. Angry you is not authorized to sign anything.

3 — WRITE THE WHOLE THING DOWN AND SEND IT TO NOBODY. Everything, unedited, ugly. Most of the pressure is wanting it heard. Paper hears it.

4 — NAME WHAT IS UNDER IT. Anger is almost always fear or shame with its chest out. Am I scared, am I embarrassed, do I feel disrespected. Naming it takes maybe 40% off the top.

5 — SAY IT TO A PERSON ONCE YOU ARE UNDER A 6 OUT OF 10. Not before. Before that you are not talking, you are detonating.

The goal is not to stop being angry. Some of it is earned. The goal is to be angry for two hours instead of deciding your whole life in the middle of it.

DO THIS TODAY

Decide right now what your anger outlet is, while you are calm. Gym, walk, bag, drive. Name one. You will not pick one well at the moment you need it.

WATCH OUT FOR

Righteous anger, the kind where you are definitely in the right. That is the most dangerous one, because being right feels like permission.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
SUN SEP 27, 2026 FREE TIME

What do I do with the free time I used to spend using?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Using ate hours. Getting it, doing it, coming down from it, hiding it. Take that out and you are handed a huge block of empty time with nothing built to fill it.

THE ANSWER

Empty time is not a bonus, it is a hazard. That hole has to get filled with something on purpose or it fills itself with the old thing.

1 — COUNT THE HOURS HONESTLY. Most people find four to six hours a day. That is a part-time job's worth of time you now have to spend.

2 — PUT SOMETHING IN THE WORST WINDOW FIRST. You know your hours. For most people it is 8pm to midnight. That window gets a plan before any other one does.

3 — PICK THINGS THAT TIRE YOU OUT. The gym, long walks, physical work, anything that ends with you actually tired. A tired body sleeps, and sleep is where the whole thing gets easier.

4 — PICK ONE THING THAT MAKES SOMETHING. A skill, a project, a car, a class, food you cook yourself. Recovery gets a lot easier when there is something to show for the days.

5 — LEAVE SOME BOREDOM IN. You are not supposed to schedule every minute forever. Learning to sit in a plain quiet hour without reaching for something is part of the work.

You are not filling time to distract yourself. You are building the life the using was standing in the way of.

DO THIS TODAY

Write down the four-hour window that is worst for you. Put one thing in it for tomorrow. Just tomorrow.

WATCH OUT FOR

Filling all of it with one thing, and that thing being another way to disappear. Ten hours of scrolling or gambling is the same hole with a different shovel.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
SAT SEP 26, 2026 THE WALL

Why do I feel worse at 30 days than I did at 3?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Everybody warns you about week one. Nobody warns you that the month mark can feel like the floor dropping out, right when everyone else has stopped clapping.

THE ANSWER

This is normal and it has a name people in the rooms use: the wall. It is not a sign it is not working. It is a sign the easy part is over.

Days 1 to 10 run on adrenaline and other people's attention. Around day 30 the adrenaline is gone, the attention is gone, and what is left is your actual life with none of the numbing on it.

1 — EXPECT IT INSTEAD OF INTERPRETING IT. Feeling flat at 30 days is not evidence you are broken or that sobriety does not work for you. It is the schedule.

2 — STOP MEASURING BY FEELINGS AND START MEASURING BY ACTIONS. Did you not use today, did you eat, did you move, did you sleep. Four boxes. Feelings lie at 30 days. Boxes do not.

3 — FIX SLEEP FIRST. Sleep comes back late and it comes back ugly. Same bedtime, no screen in the last 30 minutes, no naps after 4pm. Most of the wall is exhaustion wearing a bigger name.

4 — TELL SOMEBODY IT IS HARDER NOW. People assume you are fine at a month. They cannot read your mind and they will not check.

5 — GIVE IT TO 60. The 30 to 60 stretch is where it stops being something you are doing and starts being something you are.

DO THIS TODAY

Write today's date and the date 30 days from now on a piece of paper. That second date is the one you are walking to. Nothing before it is a verdict.

WATCH OUT FOR

The thought that you clearly have this handled now, so one would be fine. Day 30 confidence has ended more runs than day 3 pain.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
FRI SEP 25, 2026 SOCIAL CIRCLE

What do I do when my whole social circle drinks?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Everyone tells you to change your people. Nobody tells you what to do on a Saturday when changing your people means having nobody.

THE ANSWER

You do not have to burn your whole life down in week one. You do have to be honest about which of these people are friends and which ones were just there.

1 — SORT THEM INTO THREE PILES. People who would still hang out if there was no drinking. People who would not. People who actively want you drinking. The third pile is not a friendship, and you already know who is in it.

2 — KEEP PILE ONE, ON DIFFERENT TERMS. Breakfast, the gym, a drive, a game. Same people, different hours. You will find out fast who is actually your friend.

3 — GO LATE AND LEAVE EARLY WHEN YOU DO GO. Show up after the meal, leave before the second wind. Nobody tracks arrival times and nobody argues with somebody already holding their keys.

4 — DRIVE YOURSELF, EVERY TIME. This is the whole game. If getting home depends on somebody else being ready, you are not in charge of when you leave.

5 — BUILD ONE NEW THING BEFORE YOU NEED IT. A gym at a set time, a meeting, a run club, a church group. One standing thing with people who never knew the old you.

This gets lonely for a stretch. That stretch is real and it ends. Nobody tells you that part either.

DO THIS TODAY

Name the one person in your circle who would still show up if there was zero drinking. Text them today and make a plan that has nothing to do with a bar.

WATCH OUT FOR

The friend who keeps saying one won't kill you. That is not encouragement. That is somebody who needs you drinking so they do not have to look at their own.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
THU SEP 24, 2026 SPONSOR

How do I find a sponsor when I don't know anybody?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Everybody says get a sponsor like it is a thing you pick up at the store. Nobody tells you how you are supposed to walk up to a stranger and ask them to carry part of your life.

THE ANSWER

You are not asking somebody to adopt you. You are asking one person if you can call them when it gets bad. That is a much smaller ask than it feels like.

1 — GO TO THE SAME MEETING THREE TIMES. Not three different meetings. The same one. You cannot ask a room full of strangers for anything, and after three weeks it is not a room full of strangers.

2 — LISTEN FOR WHO YOU WANT TO SOUND LIKE IN A YEAR. Not the loudest person, not the funniest. The one who says something and you think, yeah, that is true. That is your shortlist.

3 — ASK SMALL. Hey, can I get your number in case I have a bad night? That is the whole sentence. Most people say yes to that instantly.

4 — USE THE NUMBER INSIDE A WEEK. A number you never call is not a sponsor, it is a contact. Call once about something small so the first call is not an emergency.

5 — IF THEY SAY NO, IT IS ABOUT THEIR CAPACITY, NOT YOUR WORTH. People sponsoring three others say no all the time. Ask the next one.

You can also do this with a temporary sponsor while you look. Most rooms will hand you one the night you ask.

DO THIS TODAY

Find one meeting that happens at the same time every week and put all four of the next four weeks in your calendar tonight.

WATCH OUT FOR

Waiting until you feel ready to ask. Ready never arrives. The people who get sponsors are the ones who asked while still feeling stupid about asking.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
WED SEP 23, 2026 RESUME-GAP

How do I explain a gap on my resume from when I was using?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Employers see the gap before they see you, and your brain fills the silence in with the worst possible interrogation. Most of that scene never happens outside your own head.

THE ANSWER

You do not owe anyone your full story. You owe them one true sentence that closes the subject and gets the conversation back to the job.

1 — WRITE ONE LINE, NOT A CONFESSION. Something like: I took time to deal with a health issue and I'm in a strong place now. True, short, closed.

2 — STOP REHEARSING THE WORST VERSION. Most interviewers never ask directly. You are preparing for a scene that plays out in maybe one interview out of ten.

3 — POINT AT WHAT YOU DID WITH THE TIME. A program, a job you held through it, a skill you picked up. Any structure counts. A gap looks worse empty than it looks explained.

4 — LET THE SILENCE SIT AFTER YOU ANSWER. Do not keep talking to fill the pause. Over-explaining is what turns one clean sentence into a confession.

The gap is a fact about your calendar. It stops being a fact about your character the moment you stop treating it like one.

DO THIS TODAY

Write your one sentence right now, say it out loud once, and put it where you'll see it before your next interview.

WATCH OUT FOR

Volunteering it before anyone asks. Answer the question they actually asked, not the one you're afraid they're thinking.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
TUE SEP 22, 2026 RELAPSE

What do I do the day after I slipped?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

The slip is rarely what ends people. The week after is. The shame says you already blew it, and the shame is a far better salesman than the drug ever was.

THE ANSWER

One day is one day. It only becomes a run if you decide today that you are already gone.

1 — EAT AND DRINK WATER FIRST. Before any thinking. You cannot make a decent decision while wrecked and dehydrated, and every thought you have right now is coming out of a body that needs food.

2 — TELL ONE PERSON TODAY. Not everyone. One. The secret is what turns a day into a month. Saying it out loud to a single human cuts the power out of it.

3 — WRITE DOWN THE HOUR IT STARTED AND WHAT CAME BEFORE IT. Not to punish yourself. That sequence is the most useful information you will get all year, and in three days you will not remember it accurately.

4 — DO ONE NORMAL THING. Go to work. Make the bed. Go to the meeting. Normal is what tells your brain the wheels did not come off.

You did not lose your progress. You lost a day. Those are different numbers and shame will try to tell you they are the same.

DO THIS TODAY

Text one person the plain sentence: I used yesterday, I am back today. No explanation attached. Send it before you talk yourself out of it.

WATCH OUT FOR

Starting Monday. Whatever today is, that is the day. Waiting for a clean start date is how one day turns into five.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
MON SEP 21, 2026 FAMILY

How do I rebuild trust with my family without begging for it?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

You want them to see you are different now, and they are still braced for the last version of you. Both of those things are fair, and the gap between them is where most people give up or blow up.

THE ANSWER

Trust is not something you ask for. It is a record you build, and they get to read it at their own speed.

1 — STOP ANNOUNCING. Every time you tell them how much you have changed, you are asking them to give you credit in advance. Say less. Let the weeks do the talking.

2 — PICK ONE SMALL PROMISE A WEEK AND KEEP IT EXACTLY. Be there at six. Call Sunday. Pay back twenty. Small and kept beats big and nearly. They are not measuring size, they are measuring reliability.

3 — BE ON TIME. It sounds like nothing. To someone who spent years not knowing where you were, on time is the entire message.

4 — LET THEM BE ANGRY WITHOUT FIXING IT. If it comes up, do not defend and do not perform remorse. That was on me. I am working on it. Then let it sit.

Months, not days. If you need them to believe you this week, you are still doing it for the relief and not for them.

DO THIS TODAY

Make one specific promise to one family member today, small enough that you cannot fail it, and write it where you will see it.

WATCH OUT FOR

Keeping score. The second you start counting what you have done and what they still owe you, you are building a case, not a relationship.

SUN SEP 20, 2026 NIGHTTIME

What do I do at night when the cravings get loudest?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Nights are worse for almost everyone and almost nobody warns you about it. You are tired, the day is done, nobody is awake, and the reasons you had at 10am are not in the room anymore.

THE ANSWER

Night cravings are mostly a tired brain and an empty room, not a real decision arriving. Treat it like weather, not a verdict.

1 — GET OUT OF THE BED OR THE CHAIR. Lying there arguing with it is how it wins. Stand up, change rooms, put a light on. Break the scene.

2 — DO SOMETHING WITH YOUR HANDS. Dishes, laundry, tidy one drawer, shower. Boring and physical beats anything clever. The point is the hands, not the task.

3 — WRITE THE TIME DOWN. Actually note what time it started. You will find it is the same window most nights, and something you can predict stops feeling like it came out of nowhere.

4 — TEXT SOMEBODY EVEN IF THEY ARE ASLEEP. Send it anyway. Putting it outside your own head takes weight off it, and the reply in the morning is worth having.

Then go to bed. You do not have to feel fine. You just have to not use before you fall asleep.

DO THIS TODAY

Set an alarm for twenty minutes before your usual bad hour and put the phone across the room. Beat it there instead of meeting it.

WATCH OUT FOR

Scrolling in the dark. Your phone at 1am is a machine for making you feel worse, and feeling worse is the thing the craving feeds on.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
SAT SEP 19, 2026 STEPWORK

How do I start a Step 4 inventory when I have been putting it off for months?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Everybody stalls on the same thing. It is not laziness. It is that the whole of your life at once is too big to look at, so you look at none of it and feel worse for another month.

THE ANSWER

You are not writing your life. You are writing one column of one page. The size is the only reason you have not started.

1 — SET A TIMER FOR TEN MINUTES. Not an hour. Ten. When it goes off you are allowed to stop even if you are mid-sentence.

2 — WRITE ONLY NAMES. One column, first names only, of people or things you resent. No explaining, no ranking, no story. Just the list. Ten minutes gets most people fifteen to thirty names.

3 — STOP THERE TODAY. That is a real Step 4 session. The columns beside it are tomorrow and the day after. Nobody does this in one sitting and the ones who try usually quit.

4 — TELL YOUR SPONSOR YOU STARTED. Not what is on it. Just that the paper exists now. Saying it out loud is what keeps it moving.

The page you have been avoiding for months takes ten minutes to begin. The avoiding has cost you more than the writing will.

DO THIS TODAY

Timer for ten minutes. One column. Names only. Go now, before you read anything else today.

WATCH OUT FOR

Trying to make it good. This is not an essay and nobody is grading it. A messy list that exists beats a perfect one you never wrote.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
FRI SEP 18, 2026 SOCIAL

What do I say when someone offers me a drink and I do not want to explain myself?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

People freeze on this one and then take the drink just to end the moment. The fear is not the drink, it is the follow-up questions and the whole table looking at you.

THE ANSWER

You do not owe anybody your story. You need one short sentence that closes the subject, and you need it in your mouth before you need it.

1 — PICK ONE LINE AND ONLY ONE. I'm good, thanks. Not tonight. I'm driving. I'm off it right now. Any of them work. What does not work is deciding in the moment.

2 — SAY IT LIGHT AND MOVE. The tone does the work. Say it the way you would decline a second slice of cake, then immediately ask them something about themselves. The subject changes itself.

3 — HAVE SOMETHING IN YOUR HAND. A soda, a water, a coffee. An empty hand invites offers. A full hand ends them before they start.

4 — IF THEY PUSH, REPEAT THE SAME WORDS. Do not add a reason, because a reason is something they can argue with. Same sentence, same tone, second time. People drop it.

The ones who keep pushing after that are telling you something about themselves, not about you.

DO THIS TODAY

Say your line out loud once, right now, in your normal voice. Out loud, not in your head. It should sound boring. If it sounds like a speech, make it shorter.

WATCH OUT FOR

Explaining. The second you start explaining you have opened a negotiation, and you are now arguing for something you already decided.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
THU SEP 17, 2026 WEEKEND

How do I get through the first weekend sober when nobody around me is?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

Weekdays have structure holding you up. Friday night takes the structure away and hands you forty-eight hours with nothing in them, surrounded by people doing the exact thing you are trying not to do.

THE ANSWER

The weekend is not a test of willpower. It is a scheduling problem. Willpower runs out around 9pm on a Friday. A plan does not.

1 — FILL FRIDAY NIGHT BEFORE FRIDAY. Not with anything impressive. A gym session at 7, a long walk, a movie you already picked. Decide it Thursday, because Friday at 6pm you will not decide anything good.

2 — HAVE AN EXIT LINE READY. If you go anywhere people are drinking, know exactly how you leave and when. I have to be up early. That is the whole sentence. Nobody follows up.

3 — PUT SATURDAY MORNING ON THE HOOK. Make a plan with someone at 9am Saturday. A gym partner, breakfast, your mother. Something that notices if you do not show.

4 — EAT. Most Friday relapses start at hungry and tired, not at wanting to use. Eat a real meal before the evening starts.

You are not trying to enjoy this weekend. You are trying to be here on Monday having not used. That is a win even if it was boring.

DO THIS TODAY

Open your calendar right now and put one thing in Friday 7pm and one thing in Saturday 9am. Two entries, two minutes.

WATCH OUT FOR

Going somewhere with no way home of your own. If leaving depends on somebody else being ready to go, you are not in charge of when you leave.

WED SEP 16, 2026 CRAVING

What do I do in the first 10 minutes of a craving?

WHY THEY ARE ASKING

A craving feels like it is going to last forever, so people go looking for a plan at the exact moment they cannot think of one. Most of what they find is call your sponsor, and that is the whole answer they get.

THE ANSWER

A craving feels permanent. It is not. It rises, it peaks, and it comes back down on its own. Your only job is to still be here when it does.

1 — MOVE, first minute. Get your body out of the room you are standing in. Not a metaphor. Outside, the bathroom, the car, anywhere. The urge is tied to where you are standing more than you think.

2 — COLD, next three minutes. Cold water on your face and your wrists. Sounds stupid until you do it. It slows you down, and slowing down takes the edge off the panic riding underneath the craving.

3 — RATE IT, next five minutes. Say a number out loud, 1 to 10. Say it again two minutes later. Watch the number move. Seeing an 8 drop to a 6 is how your brain learns this ends without you using.

4 — TELL ONE PERSON, last minute. Not to get talked down. Just so somebody knows where you are right now.

You do not have to want it to be over. You just have to be somewhere else when it is.

DO THIS TODAY

Pick your cold spot and your one person before you need them. Put both in your phone right now, in a note called TEN MINUTES.

WATCH OUT FOR

Waiting until the craving hits to decide what to do. Every step above takes five seconds to plan and feels impossible to plan mid-wave. Decide now, use it later.

— Joey, Redemption Fitness
