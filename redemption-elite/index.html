What We Actually Rebuild

Three things, and the gym is only one of them.

Anybody can hand you a training split. A split does not survive a bad month. We build all three at once, because that is what makes it hold.

Body

Training built around your gym, your schedule and your injuries. Food you can run at your real life. Strength you can see in the mirror and measure on the bar, with photos and numbers every four weeks to prove it.

Mind

The discipline underneath the training: how you get up, how you eat when nobody is watching, how you handle the week everything goes wrong. Your Day count starts on day one and the standard stops being up for negotiation.

Spirit

Why you are doing this and who you are doing it for. The part most coaches skip, and the only part that keeps you in the gym at week nine when the newness has worn off and life gets loud.
